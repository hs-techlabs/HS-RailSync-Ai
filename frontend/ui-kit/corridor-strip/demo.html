What this page does

Hover the strip to open the lens. Hover a dot for its card, click to pin, Esc to release. The dots come from raw API-shaped records (worker_requests.json and pending_demands.json) run through CORRIDOR_MAP — so what you see is the integration path, not pre-cooked data.
